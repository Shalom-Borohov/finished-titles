Scope: the whole app (Home, type lists, add and edit sheet, stats, settings). Mode: Operate.

Audience and job: one owner logging a finished title once, at the end of an evening, then browsing their history. Constraints: Hebrew RTL with LTR titles, iPhone PWA built on Ionic iOS mode.

Chosen direction: the owner picked home tiles and the "Soft and light" style from the canvas, after rejecting black poster grids for their structure and colors. Memorable moment: the Home screen, where a fan of recent posters on each type tile shows the whole collection at a glance.

Unresolved: dark mode hasn't been raised with the owner.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Your whole collection across every medium, at a glance, in a calm light room. Refuses the category's black poster wall and its one endless list.

OWN-WORLD: Pale lavender ground #EEF0FA, white rounded tiles, one violet accent #5B4BDB, navy ink #1E2140, Rubik at regular, medium and bold. Everything has round corners; soft offset shadows; posters are the only saturated color.

STORY: The owner opens the app, sees totals and recent posters per type, taps a tile or the search field to find something, and taps plus to log what they just finished with a rating in words.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Large title and tagline with total at the top, violet round add button on the leading-opposite side, a white search pill beneath, a two-column grid of six type tiles each with a three-poster fan above its name and violet count, a slim Other row, and a floating white pill tab bar at the bottom.

FORM: Standing exit taken by the owner, then a surface structure they chose from sketches: home tiles over a standard iOS tab shell, on the owner's chosen palette. Signature move: the rotated three-poster fan on each tile. seed:bfec09c59112e31c

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
